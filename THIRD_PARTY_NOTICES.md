# Third-party components

## Noto Animated Emoji — Google

Catalog: https://googlefonts.github.io/noto-emoji-animation/

Data: https://googlefonts.github.io/noto-emoji-animation/data/api.json

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), as stated in the official catalog FAQ. https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode

The editor includes a catalog snapshot of 881 animation identifiers. Animation files are requested from fonts.gstatic.com only when browsing/importing the catalog. Selected GIFs are stored in projects and exports. GIF comments may be removed; frame image data, timings, and loop instructions are retained. Attribution and license information accompany imported images and are shown in the exported game's animation sources disclosure.
